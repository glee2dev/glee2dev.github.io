Proportion, by segment

GP22: 1,480 car side profiles across 37 brands and 10 segments, with a detector for the six design features that define a profile — body, bodyside, cabin, daylight opening, wheel, tire — at 0.995 mAP. First author, CVPR Workshops 2022. Now becoming an interactive 3D proportion tool, a single HTML file, that lets you feel why a Golf and a 3 Series are not the same shape and where the line between segments actually falls.

Paper, arXivDataset, ZenodoTool in progress
