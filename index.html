Who did the math?

When an LLM does the analysis, it invents the numbers. When it only narrates numbers you computed, it doesn't. Ten questions on a synthetic 10,000-household vehicle panel, three ways of answering, every number checked — eight runs across five labs. The naive-grounded model read individual records perfectly and got every aggregate wrong. The same model run twice moved more than two different labs did.

Proportion, by segment

GP22: 1,480 car side profiles across 37 brands and 10 segments, with a detector for the six design features that define a profile — body, bodyside, cabin, daylight opening, wheel, tire — at 0.995 mAP. First author, CVPR Workshops 2022. Now an interactive proportion tool: one side-profile line, the six GP22 boxes, every length in tire diameters, fourteen real cars as presets you can morph between.
