How I work

In car design, one line of light describes the whole form and everything else is left out. I build data products the same way: one claim, one build that tests it, one number that says whether it held. If a number can't be checked, it isn't in the answer. If a feature doesn't carry the argument, it isn't on the page.

Ten years in transportation design in Detroit and Turin, a master's at KAIST on deep learning for design, and since then production data science and AI engineering — computer vision on factory lines, multi-agent analytics systems, synthetic data pipelines. The design training is what makes the systems legible; the engineering is what makes the claims checkable.
